How can this framework be attacked?

It can be attacked via a man-in-the-middle because it lacks identity verifications of 
client/server. An attacker could insert their own public keys in the middle of the key 
exchange and read/modify all messages sent.

Replay attacks are also possible because an old message copied from the network could 
be hashed and sent again. Another issue is lack of digital signatures, so we cannot 
ensure responses are from who they say they are from. Finally, it is unencrypted traffic 
running over raw TCP.

How would you make the framework more secure?

The framework could use some sort of authentication to verify client/server identities. 
This would prevent a man-in-the-middle from getting involved in the conversation. 
Timestamps or nonces could be added to prevent replay attacks.

I would use TLS as the transport protocol because it already implements encryption. 
Session management and key management would also help.
